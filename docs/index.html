Experimental Overview (NASA APEX-03-1 / Veggie Hardware)

Wild-type Arabidopsis thaliana (Col-0) seedlings were germinated and grown aboard the International Space Station (ISS) inside the Veggie plant growth facility during SpaceX CRS-5 (Expedition 42). Synchronous ground controls were maintained in the ISS Environmental Simulator (ISSES) chamber at Kennedy Space Center with an identical 48-hour delayed profile. Root cell walls were extracted sequentially with CDTA, $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$, 1M KOH, 4M KOH, acidic chlorite, and post-chlorite 4M KOH to comprehensively profile non-cellulosic polysaccharides with 155 glycan-directed monoclonal antibodies.
